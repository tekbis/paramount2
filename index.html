Bespoke experiences

Plan an experience
Hillside Residence · Islamabad

Margalla Hills, Trail 3

Climb through shaded oak and pine on Islamabad’s favourite morning hike. At the top, the capital and the white minarets of the Faisal Mosque spread out below you.

Central Residence · Karachi

Seafood by the Arabian Sea

Grilled prawns, fresh fish and warm naan at Do Darya’s waterfront restaurants, where tables sit right above the waves. Come for sunset and stay for the sea breeze.

Mountain Terrace · Khaira Gali

Ayubia chairlift

Glide above the pine forests of Ayubia National Park, a short drive from Khaira Gali. The ride opens up sweeping views across the Galyat hills.

The Residence · Lahore

Dinner facing the Badshahi Mosque

The rooftop restaurants of Fort Road Food Street look straight onto the illuminated Badshahi Mosque. Order karahi and fresh naan as the old city lights up.

Hillside Residence · Islamabad

A slow afternoon at Rawal Lake

Pack a picnic for the shores of Rawal Lake, framed by the Margalla Hills. Lake View Park makes it an easy, unhurried escape from the city.

The Residence · Lahore

Walk the Walled City

Enter through the old gates and follow the Royal Trail past havelis, bazaars and centuries-old mosques. It’s the best way to meet Lahore’s Mughal heart on foot.

Central Residence · Karachi

Sunset on Karachi’s coastline

Leave the city behind for the golden sand and rocky coves of beaches like French Beach and Cape Monze. Barefoot walks and blazing sunsets come standard.

Mountain Terrace · Khaira Gali

Pipeline Track walk

An easy, mostly level trail that winds through cool pine forest between Ayubia and Dunga Gali. There are views across the valleys at almost every turn.

Mountain Terrace · Khaira Gali

Bonfire nights in the hills

Barbecue on the lawn, a crackling fire and the lights of the hill stations twinkling below. Our 24/7 chef takes care of everything else.
