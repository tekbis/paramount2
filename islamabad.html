Around the residence

Nearby areas & activities

Hillside Residence sits close to some of Islamabad’s best-loved landmarks. The Faisal Mosque, framed by the Margalla Hills, is a short drive away.

For fresh air, Margalla Hills National Park offers hiking trails and scenic walks right on the city’s doorstep.

01Bespoke experience · Islamabad

Margalla Hills, Trail 3

Climb through shaded oak and pine on Islamabad’s favourite morning hike. At the top, the capital and the white minarets of the Faisal Mosque spread out below you.

02Bespoke experience · Islamabad

A slow afternoon at Rawal Lake

Pack a picnic for the shores of Rawal Lake, framed by the Margalla Hills. Lake View Park makes it an easy, unhurried escape from the city.
